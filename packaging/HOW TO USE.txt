AWESOMEBOOKS SCRAPER PRO - HOW TO USE
=====================================

WHAT THIS PC NEEDS
  - Windows 10 or 11
  - An internet connection
  - Python 3.10 or newer (if it is missing, setup.bat installs it for you)

FIRST TIME ONLY
  1. Extract this ZIP to a permanent folder with a short path, for example
     C:\AwesomeBooks Scraper Pro
     (not the Downloads folder, and do not run it from inside the ZIP).
  2. Double-click  setup.bat  and wait for "Setup complete" (about 1-2 minutes).

EVERY TIME
  1. Double-click  run.bat
  2. A black window opens and the dashboard opens in your browser
     (http://127.0.0.1:7642).
  3. KEEP THE BLACK WINDOW OPEN while a job is running.
     - Closing the browser tab is fine: the job keeps running.
     - Closing the black window stops the scraper.

PRICES ARE ALWAYS FOR THE UNITED STATES
  AwesomeBooks asks every new visitor for a shipping country and shows a
  20%-off pop-up. You never have to deal with either: the scraper picks
  "Ship to United States" by itself and checks that every page says
  SHIP TO US and USD before it records a price. Nothing opens on screen.

PAGE 1 - CATALOG EXTRACTOR (scrape a whole section)
  1. Open the section on awesomebooks.com (a category, Used, Bargain,
     New & Trending, Bestsellers, a search...) and copy its address.
  2. Paste it into "Section link" (or click one of the quick buttons).
  3. Optional: type a number into "Max books" to stop after that many books.
     Leave it empty to read the whole section, every page.
  4. Press "Start extraction". When it finishes, the Excel file is saved to
     your Downloads folder automatically.

PAGE 2 - STOCK MONITOR (check a list of ISBNs)
  1. Click "Download upload template" on the page (or use
     ISBN_Upload_Template.xlsx in this folder).
  2. Put ONE ISBN PER ROW in the "ISBN" column. Delete the example rows.
     ISBN-10s are fine: they are converted to ISBN-13. Other columns are ignored.
  3. Drag the file onto the upload box, then press "Start stock check".
  4. When it finishes, the Excel file is saved to your Downloads folder
     automatically, in the same row order as your file.

THE EXCEL COLUMNS
  - ISBN-13 only (AwesomeBooks also shows an ISBN-10; it is not used).
  - New Available / New Price (USD) / New Qty
  - Used Available / Used Price (USD) / Used Qty
    Each condition has its own price and its own stock, exactly as a shopper
    sees them after clicking the NEW or USED button on the book's page.
  - Quantity (stock check) = New Qty + Used Qty. 0 = out of stock or not listed.
  - Quantities are exact. The website shows "20+" to shoppers above 20; the
    scraper reads the real number from the site's own product data.

GOOD TO KNOW
  - Only one job runs at a time.
  - Starting a new run replaces the previous run on that page. Excel files that
    were already saved are never deleted.
  - Internet dropped? The job waits and continues by itself.
  - Power cut or PC restarted? Double-click run.bat again and the job continues
    automatically from where it stopped.
  - Pressed Stop? Press Resume to continue.
  - Speed is about 1 book every 2 seconds on "Balanced" (each book is opened
    as New and as Used). This is on purpose, so the website never blocks the
    scraper. Very large sections (Used has ~930,000 books) take days; use
    "Max books" or a narrower section when you only need part of it.
  - Prices can differ by a few cents between two visits: AwesomeBooks converts
    its prices to dollars with a rate that moves slightly. The Excel file holds
    exactly what the site showed at the moment each book was checked.
